Polish phonetics (M10): eight representations in two topics — `consonants` (six consonant sounds
that do not exist, or are easy to mispronounce, in English) and `vowels` (the two nasal vowels).
Each file is one topic and its representations; see
[content/languages/README.md](../../README.md) and
[docs/architecture/content-architecture.md](../../../../docs/architecture/content-architecture.md)
for the format and validation.

This is a seed set, not a complete phonemic inventory of Polish: it demonstrates the phonetics
system's model (IPA, description, example words, per-student progress), it is not a claim of CEFR
coverage or a substitute for a pronunciation course.

**Sources.** The IPA transcriptions and articulatory descriptions follow the standard academic
description of Polish phonology (consonant/vowel inventories and their IPA values as documented in
peer-reviewed phonetic literature and standard phonology references, e.g. Jassem (2003), "Polish",
_Journal of the International Phonetic Association_, and the phoneme inventory cross-checked
against Rocławski's _Zarys fonologii, fonetyki, fonotaktyki i fonostatystyki współczesnego języka
polskiego_) — not invented or guessed. Example words and their explanatory notes are original
wording. No content was copied from a textbook, course or other protected source.
