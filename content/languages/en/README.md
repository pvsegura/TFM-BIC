# content/languages/en

English for Spanish speakers — the second supported language (M22). `language.json` registers it (`en`,
`English`, `en-GB`, `ltr`) with A1 `available` and A2–C2 `planned`. All explanations, translations, exercise
instructions and video narration are in Spanish (`instructionLanguage: "es"`); the English phrases are spoken
by the characters.

Like the Polish seed, A1 is a small, representative **seed set** — five lessons/explanations, nine exercises,
29 words in six categories and six phonetic representations — not a complete A1 course and not
CEFR-certified. Sound comparisons with Spanish are approximate and say so.
