# M16 — Security Risk Register

Status: M16, 2026-09-26. Security-specific risks of the current implementation. Project-level
risks stay in [docs/risk-register.md](../risk-register.md). Levels are descriptive
(CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW) and reasoned in the [audit](M16-SECURITY-AUDIT.md); no numeric scores
are used. "Risk" is the level **before** M16's remediation; the status column says what is left.

| ID    | Area                   | Threat                                                                                                                        | Likelihood                                         | Impact                                | Risk   | Existing control                                  | Remediation                                                                                | Status                    |
| ----- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| SR-01 | Rate limiting / proxy  | All clients share one proxy IP → one attacker locks everyone out of login; or spoofed `X-Forwarded-For` bypasses limits       | High once deployed behind any proxy                | High (auth unavailable / brute force) | HIGH   | Per-IP limits                                     | `TRUST_PROXY` IP/CIDR allowlist, validated; deployment checklist                           | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-02 | Authentication         | Distributed password guessing against one account                                                                             | Medium                                             | High (account takeover)               | MEDIUM | Argon2id, per-IP limit                            | Per-account login limit (normalised email)                                                 | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-03 | Account deletion       | Stolen session guesses the password from many IPs, then deletes the account                                                   | Low                                                | High (irreversible loss)              | MEDIUM | Password + confirm, per-IP limit                  | Per-user limit                                                                             | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-04 | AI generation          | Cost abuse from many IPs; shared-NAT classes starved                                                                          | Medium once a paid provider is on                  | Medium (cost / availability)          | MEDIUM | Per-IP limit, cache, concurrency cap              | Per-user limits                                                                            | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-05 | Tokens                 | Concurrent reuse of a reset/verification token; partial state after a failure                                                 | Low                                                | Medium                                | MEDIUM | Hashed 256-bit tokens, expiry, `usedAt`           | Atomic consume-before-change                                                               | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-06 | HTTP hardening         | Clickjacking of settings/deletion, MIME sniffing, token leak via `Referer`, private responses cached, no CSP backstop for XSS | Medium                                             | Medium                                | MEDIUM | React escaping, per-route no-store on most routes | API header hook + no-store default + HSTS (prod); SPA CSP/header policy, E2E on prod build | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-07 | Error handling         | Misleading 4xx bodies; any client floods error-level logs                                                                     | High                                               | Low                                   | LOW    | Generic 500                                       | Status-specific safe bodies; 4xx at info                                                   | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-08 | Information disclosure | Path reflected in 404; no correlation id                                                                                      | High                                               | Low                                   | LOW    | JSON encoding                                     | Generic 404; UUID `X-Request-Id`                                                           | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-09 | Configuration          | Weak session secret or localhost/HTTP base URL accepted in production                                                         | Medium                                             | Medium                                | MEDIUM | Secret required in prod                           | ≥ 32 chars; `APP_BASE_URL` required + HTTPS                                                | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-10 | Resource limits        | Slow-request DoS; 1 MiB bodies on auth routes                                                                                 | Medium without a proxy                             | Medium                                | LOW    | Per-route limits on most writes                   | `requestTimeout`; 16 KiB default body limit                                                | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-11 | Secrets                | `.env.production` etc. committed by mistake                                                                                   | Medium                                             | High                                  | MEDIUM | `.env`, `.env.local` ignored                      | Ignore `.env.*` except the example                                                         | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-12 | Local infrastructure   | Dev database reachable from the LAN with a committed password                                                                 | Low                                                | Low (dev data only)                   | LOW    | Dev-only credentials                              | Bind to 127.0.0.1                                                                          | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-13 | CSRF                   | Request without `Origin` from an old or unusual client                                                                        | Low                                                | Medium                                | LOW    | SameSite=Strict, JSON only, Origin check          | `Sec-Fetch-Site` fallback                                                                  | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-14 | Rate limiting          | Profile endpoints unbounded                                                                                                   | Low                                                | Low                                   | LOW    | Auth required                                     | Read/write limits                                                                          | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-15 | Configuration          | Test-only rate-limit relaxation enabled in production                                                                         | Low                                                | Medium                                | LOW    | Documented as test-only                           | Refuse outside `NODE_ENV=test`                                                             | Planned (M16)             |
| SR-16 | Supply chain           | Vulnerable dependency or committed secret not caught by CI                                                                    | Medium                                             | High                                  | MEDIUM | Frozen lockfile, manual audits                    | `pnpm audit --prod` stage; secret scanning PENDING                                         | Planned (M16), partial    |
